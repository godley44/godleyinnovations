Who this is between

Justin Godley, doing business as Plumbline Social, in Maryland ("Plumbline Social", "we").

The business and owner named at checkout ("you"). Your start date is the date of your first payment.

When Godley Innovations Studio, LLC is formed, Plumbline Social's side of this agreement moves to that company. Nothing else changes for you.

1. What we do

  • We run your Facebook and Instagram from your real job photos. 16 posts a month.
  • You approve each week's posts before anything goes live.
  • We answer comments and messages as your business. Anything about price or scheduling goes to you. We never answer it in your name.
  • We build and manage your ad campaigns. You pay Meta directly for ad spend. No markup.
  • We set up missed-call text-back on a phone number Plumbline Social provides.
  • We send you one plain-English report a month.

2. What you do

  • Send us photos from your jobs.
  • Reply to the weekly approval text.
  • Give Plumbline Social access to your Facebook Page and Instagram.
  • You keep ownership of every account.

3. Price and payment

  • You are one of our first three clients, so you get the founding rate.
  • Months 1 to 7 (your first 30 days, then six more months): $697 a month.
  • From month 8: our standard rate of $997 a month, month to month.
  • You authorize Plumbline Social to charge your card each month through Stripe until this agreement ends.

4. Ending the agreement

  • First 30 days: either of us can cancel, and nothing more is owed.
  • After that: either of us can end this any time with 30 days' written notice. A text or email is enough. You pay for that final month and nothing more.
  • If you come back later, the standard rate applies.

5. The founding trade

  • If you are satisfied, you give an honest testimonial.
  • You also allow Plumbline Social to publish your results (calls, messages, quote requests) with your business name.
  • Whenever we publish your testimonial or results, we say you are a founding client who received a discounted rate.
  • You can take back permission to publish anything new at any time, by text or email.

6. No guarantees

  • We do not promise any number of jobs, calls or followers.
  • Plumbline Social never quotes prices or books jobs in your name.

7. Ownership and exit

  • You own your accounts, photos and posts.
  • Our access ends the day this agreement ends.
  • The text-back number can be transferred to you at cost.

8. Basics

  • Maryland law applies. Anne Arundel County, Maryland.
